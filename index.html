A few things to know

Simple by design.

Is this another AI chatbot?

No. ChatTiles is a shortcut widget for conversations and projects in ChatGPT. It does not generate answers or include an AI subscription.

Does it work with private chats?

You can save a private conversation’s browser address. Opening it still requires access to the correct ChatGPT account. Adding a shortcut does not publish or share the conversation.

Can I use several widgets?

Yes. All current widget instances display the same saved list. Resize each instance to suit your home screen.

Can I download it now?

ChatTiles is being prepared for Google Play. The download link will appear here after it becomes available. For testing enquiries, contact the developer.

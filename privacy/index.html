Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026 · Covers ChatTiles version 0.1.0

ChatTiles: AI Chat Widget is an Android application developed by Eduard Dubilyer. It stores shortcuts to ChatGPT chats and projects on your device.

The current app has no developer-operated server, advertising, analytics, accounts, or in-app purchases. It does not download or read your conversations.

1. Information stored by the app

When you save a shortcut, ChatTiles stores its name, provider, selected color, destination link, and a generated identifier in the app’s private local storage. It also stores an action marker for new-chat shortcuts. This information is used to display, edit, remove, and open your shortcuts.

ChatTiles does not request your ChatGPT password or access your conversation history, messages, contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

2. Installed ChatGPT information

ChatTiles checks for the installed ChatGPT app to display its application icon and to find a supported new-chat action. This lookup is limited to the ChatGPT package. ChatTiles does not upload an inventory of your installed applications.

3. Opening links and external apps

When you tap a shortcut, ChatTiles passes its destination link or new-chat action to an external application. Android may open ChatGPT or your browser, depending on the action and your device settings. Link help can also open ChatGPT’s website in Chrome.

The destination service may process the link, your account information, and connection information under its own privacy policy. See OpenAI’s Privacy Policy. ChatTiles does not control those services. A saved private-chat link does not grant access to someone who lacks permission to view that conversation.

4. Collection, sharing, and advertising

The current Android app does not send your saved shortcut list to the developer, sell it, use advertising SDKs, or use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The external-app actions described above occur when you choose to open a shortcut or help link.

5. Retention and deletion

Shortcuts remain on your device until you remove them, clear the app’s storage through Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Removing a widget alone does not delete the saved list. Deleting a shortcut does not delete the underlying ChatGPT conversation or project.

The app disables Android cloud backup through its manifest. Device transfer and backup behavior can also depend on your Android version and device manufacturer. ChatTiles does not provide cloud synchronization or a developer-held backup.

6. Security

Shortcut data is kept in Android app-private storage. Links can identify private conversations, so avoid sending them in public bug reports or screenshots. Device security and access to your ChatGPT account remain under your control.

7. Support messages

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send passwords or private conversation contents. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your enquiry, follow-up support, or applicable obligations. You can request deletion by email.

8. This website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, forms, or third-party font requests. If published on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes hosting-related information, including visitor IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links and email services have their own privacy practices.

9. Children

ChatTiles is a general-purpose shortcut utility and is not specifically directed at children. It does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. Use of ChatGPT is subject to that service’s age requirements and terms.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change. The date and covered version above identify the current policy. Planned paid features are not part of the version covered here; this policy must be reviewed before purchases or additional services are introduced.

A shortcut to getting started

Your home screen,
with your chats.

This guide covers the current ChatTiles app. Android 6.0 or later is required. Widget menus and link handling vary between launchers and devices.

1. Add the widget

  1. Install ChatTiles and open it once.
  2. Touch and hold an empty area on your Android home screen.
  3. Open Widgets, find ChatTiles: AI Chat Widget, and drag it onto your home screen.
  4. Tap the widget’s + button to add your first shortcut.

2. Copy a conversation link

For a shortcut back to your own conversation, copy its browser address:

  1. Open chatgpt.com in Chrome and sign in to your account.
  2. Open the conversation you want, including a conversation inside a project.
  3. Use Chrome’s menu ⋮ → Share → Copy link, or copy the address from the address bar.
  4. Paste that address into ChatTiles.

Use the browser address to return to a private conversation. ChatGPT’s own Share feature creates a shared link with different access and privacy behavior. You do not need to make a conversation public to save a shortcut.

3. Name it and choose a color

Enter a name of up to 80 characters, choose ChatGPT and one of the eight colors, paste the link, and save. Your shortcut appears in the widget. Short names are easier to recognize on a small home screen.

4. Add a project or a new-chat action

For a project, copy the project page’s browser address. You can also use the project’s sharing controls when available, paying attention to the access settings you choose. Paste the project link into the same form.

For a new conversation, open the General tab, choose a name and color, and select Add new-chat icon. This action requires ChatGPT to be installed and enabled in the same Android profile, with a supported new-chat action.

5. Edit, replace, or remove

Open ChatTiles, or tap + on the widget, and choose Saved chats. Select Edit to change a name, color, or destination. Saving an edit preserves the shortcut’s position. Select Remove to delete only the shortcut; the original conversation stays in ChatGPT.

6. Resize your grid

Touch and hold the widget and adjust its resize handles. The grid uses one to five columns, depending on its width. More shortcuts can be reached by scrolling. All current widget instances share the same list.

If a shortcut does not open as expected

  • A browser opens: ordinary chat and project links follow Android’s app-link settings. Check ChatGPT’s supported-link settings under Android Settings → Apps.
  • A private chat is unavailable: confirm that the app or browser is signed in to an account with access.
  • New chat is unavailable: check that ChatGPT is installed in the same Android profile and supports the action.
  • The widget has not refreshed: open ChatTiles once. If needed, remove and re-add the widget; your saved list remains.
